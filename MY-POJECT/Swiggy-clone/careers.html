How We Hire?

At Swiggy, we're always looking for people who can bring new perspectives and life experiences to our teams. If you're looking for a place that values your curiosity, passion and desire to learn, you're in the right place.

5 Steps to become a Swiggster

Apply

See something you want to be part of? Apply using the careers portal.

Written / Technical

If we like your profile, we'll invite you for a written or technical assessment.

Personal Interview

Meet our hiring team, discuss your skills, projects and experience.

HR Discussion

Discuss compensation, benefits and ask any questions you have.

Final Review

We'll get back to you after the hiring process with the final decision.
